01 · Get paid

It gets invoices out clean, then chases the late ones for you.

A prospect texts, calls, or fills out your form at 2pm on a Tuesday. Coffer answers immediately, in your voice, from your own knowledge base — then books the consultation straight into your calendar. It won't quote a price, promise a timeline, or commit to scope. Those come to you.

Fair questions.

The ones that actually decide it. Anything else, ask us on the demo.

Will it text my clients something embarrassing?+

Not unless you approve it. Every message is a draft until you say otherwise, and you can mark any client "always ask me" permanently.

Does this replace my bookkeeper or accountant?+

No. It replaces the data entry they charge you for and the chasing nobody does. Your bookkeeper gets a login and usually less to clean up at month end.

What if the AI gets a lead's question wrong?+

It's built to defer, not improvise. On price, scope, or timeline it says it'll confirm with you and books the call. You see every conversation.

Do I have to switch accounting software?+

No. Coffer sits on top of QuickBooks or Xero. If you're on something else, ask us — we're adding integrations.

We're only four people. Is this overkill?+

Three to twenty is exactly who this is for. Bigger firms have someone whose job this is. You don't — that's the point.

Can I turn parts off?+

Yes. Run just the invoicing side, just the lead side, or all of it. Most people start with one.
